01 / Selected work

Systems I built end to end

Live Zaza AI · Founding Engineer · 2025–2026

ZazaVoice: a voice AI platform for businesses

A multi-tenant B2B platform where businesses deploy AI agents for inbound support, appointment booking and outbound campaigns. I was the only engineer, covering architecture, telephony, billing and the mobile softphone.

What I shipped

  • Streaming STT, LLM and TTS over Twilio bidirectional Media Streams, with barge-in when the caller interrupts
  • A second, lower-latency speech-to-speech path on the Gemini Live API, resampling 8 kHz μ-law to and from 16/24 kHz PCM in real time
  • Live-call function calling for availability checks, appointment booking with a double-booking guard, contact capture and voicemail detection
  • Human handoff over Twilio Conference with AI or voicemail fallback, plus a React Native softphone

Hard problems solved

  • Replaced Twilio's answering-machine detection with a listen-first gate: it greets after 1.2 s of silence or a human hello and hangs up on voicemail or IVR
  • Campaign dialer with multi-phone retries and TCPA calling-hour limits; fixed a retry bug that dialed one lead dozens of times
  • Fixed production races: a webhook vs dispatcher insert race (unique constraint), idempotent Stripe events and atomic SQL wallet debits
